### #678 (03:03Z) — [design] ε as cross-domain correspondence detector

Filed by κ. Three design components, each pinned against a known failure mode of the substrate:

1. **Finite failure-mode catalogue (the tractable core).** *Not* open-ended analogy hunting — the substrate over-generates plausible-but-spurious analogies, so open discovery is intractable. Instead a **finite** list of portable failure *shapes* (premature quotient, missing morphism action, production/detection confound, "claim cites evidence the reader's capabilities couldn't obtain" = #674). Any cell lints its matter against the list. Framed as the cross-domain generalization of negative fixtures: a negative fixture already *is* a failure shape, currently scoped to one domain. "Check against N known shapes" is a **lint pass**, not discovery.
2. **Correspondence-claim refutation discipline (the inversion that matters most).** The bottleneck is not *generating* correspondences — the substrate does that indefinitely and worthlessly. So the adversarial pressure belongs on the **claim of correspondence**, not the domain matter. A claim of the form "X's efference-copy confound *is* Y's I11" must carry its own **refutation surface**: the specific structural feature that must hold, and what would show the match is merely superficial. Same discipline as content-hashed quotes in receipts — the claim carries its own refutation. β adversarialism, pointed at the match rather than the matter.
3. **Diversity-over-depth (allocation principle).** Empirically, what catches cross-frame error is not a *deeper* cell but **two same-level readers of different failure modes disagreeing.** Lineage/failure-mode diversity at the same scope beats deeper recursion — and is cheaper than building a new role. Cited evidence: the external, different-lineage β caught over PR #672 (R11→R17) what cnos self-review structurally could not.

Two honest scope carve-outs the issue makes against itself: the **pairing/curation** of maximally-distant-yet-methodologically-related domains resists automation (similarity heuristics select for *too close*, where matches come free and worthless) and stays a human/operator act; and a required `analogues_checked` field **Goodharts** into filled-in noise unless every entry carries component 2's refutation discipline.
